05 — Interactive

Live it yourself

Ice Hockey Career &
Trajectory Simulation

All of these career pathways are deliberately designed to reflect real scenarios collected through 150+ global interviews, questionnaires and chat sessions with hockey players around the world. There are no right or wrong choices — just go with your gut.

You are born in China, where ice hockey is nowhere near as popular as in Northern Europe or North America. Spend 10 talent points, then survive the U16 Cliff. Once you quit hockey, you are out.

⏱️ 5–8 minutes 🎯 10 talent points 🎲 9 rounds, ages 0–18 🏆 18 endings 🖥️ Full screen

Keyboard supported — A/B/C to choose, Enter to continue, Esc to exit.

Design notes

Why a simulation?

A research paper can state that "academic pressure causes a 16-year-old dropout cliff". It is much harder to convey what it feels like to be the person making that decision — with a coach on one side, a principal on the other, and a family budget that does not stretch.

Every round in this simulation is drawn from a real scenario reported in the interviews: the cost of the first pair of skates, the teacher's phone call, the province with fifteen players in your age group, the contract that overlaps with school hours. The hidden rules matter too — if you spent nothing on family background, the game ends the moment you are born, because for a great many children in southern provinces, it effectively does.

Most endings are losses. That is not pessimism; it is the participation data. Fewer than 2,000 of 58,000 players are still going after sixteen.

🎯 Talents that actually matter

Family background, mental resilience and physical toughness carry real thresholds. The other four traits are offered — and, deliberately, change nothing.

🚪 Doors that close

Quit once and the run is over. There is no rewind, because in a talent pool this small there rarely is one.

🔬 Endings tied to findings

Each ending closes with the research finding it illustrates, so the story stays connected to the evidence.
